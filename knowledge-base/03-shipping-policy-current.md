# Shipping Policy

## Domestic shipping (US & Canada)

Standard shipping takes 4–7 business days and costs $5.95, free on
orders over $75. Row+ members get free standard shipping on every
order regardless of order size.

Expedited shipping (2–3 business days) is available for $14.95.

## Order processing time

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days before they ship.
Processing time is separate from transit time shown at checkout.

## Carriers

We ship via UPS and USPS depending on destination and package size.
Once an order ships, a tracking number is emailed to the customer and
is also visible via the order lookup tool.

## Order changes

Once an order has shipped, the shipping address and shipping speed
can no longer be changed. Address changes are only possible while an
order is still in "processing" status, and must go through support.

## Lost packages

If a tracking number shows no movement for 7 or more calendar days,
customers should contact support so we can open a carrier trace.
