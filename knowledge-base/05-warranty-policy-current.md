# Warranty Policy

## Coverage

All Aster & Row hard goods (bags, tumblers, hardware) carry a
**2-year limited warranty** against manufacturing defects — things
like broken zippers, failed seams, or coating that fails under normal
use. Soft goods (apparel) carry a **1-year limited warranty**.

## What is not covered

Normal wear and tear, damage from misuse, damage from improper
cleaning (see the Product Care guide for care instructions per
product), and cosmetic changes like fading from sun exposure are not
covered.

## Filing a warranty claim

Customers should contact support with their order ID, a description
of the defect, and photos. Approved claims are resolved via repair,
replacement, or store credit at Aster & Row's discretion.

## Warranty is independent of the return window

Warranty coverage applies for the full 1–2 year period regardless of
whether the standard return window (see Returns Policy) has expired.
