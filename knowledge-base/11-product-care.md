# Product Care Guide

General care instructions by product category.

## Backpacks and bags

Spot clean with a damp cloth and mild soap. Do not machine wash. Air
dry only; do not tumble dry, as heat can damage waterproof coatings.

## Apparel

Machine wash cold, tumble dry low, unless a care label says
otherwise. Avoid bleach and fabric softener on technical fabrics.

## Drinkware (tumblers and bottles)

All Aster & Row drinkware, including the Breeze Tumbler line, should
be **hand washed only**. The double-wall vacuum insulation and the
powder-coated exterior finish are not dishwasher-safe — dishwasher
detergent and heat can strip the coating and, over time, compromise
the vacuum seal. Use warm soapy water and a bottle brush for the
interior.

## Hardware (buckles, zippers)

Wipe down with a dry cloth. Do not submerge metal hardware in water
for extended periods to avoid corrosion.
