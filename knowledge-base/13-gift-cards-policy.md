# Gift Cards Policy

## Purchasing

Digital gift cards are available in denominations of $25, $50, $100,
and $250, and are delivered by email, typically within minutes.

## Redemption

Gift cards do not expire and have no fees. They can be applied at
checkout alongside one promo code, but cannot be combined with
another gift card in the same order — only one gift card code may be
applied per order.

## Returns and gift cards

Refunds for orders paid partly or fully with a gift card are issued
back to the gift card balance, not to the original payment method,
for the portion covered by the gift card.

## Lost gift card codes

If a customer loses a gift card code, support can look up the
balance and reissue the code if the original purchase order can be
verified.
