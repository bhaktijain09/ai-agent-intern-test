# Row+ Membership Benefits

Row+ is Aster & Row's paid membership tier, $49/year.

## Benefits

- Free standard shipping on every order, no minimum
- Extended 60-day return window (vs. 30 days for regular customers)
- Free return shipping (vs. $6.95 for regular customers)
- Early access to new product drops, typically 48 hours before
  general release
- 10% member discount on full-price items, stackable with one
  promotional code per order

## Tiers

There are two account tiers referenced in our systems: `standard` and
`row_plus`. There is no third "VIP" or "elite" tier at this time.

## Cancelling membership

Row+ can be cancelled any time from account settings. Benefits remain
active until the end of the current paid year; membership fees are
non-refundable for partial years.
