# Breeze Tumbler — Product Card

## Overview

The Breeze Tumbler is a 20oz double-wall vacuum-insulated tumbler
with a powder-coated exterior, available in six colors. Keeps drinks
cold for up to 24 hours and hot for up to 8 hours.

## Materials

18/8 stainless steel body, BPA-free push-button lid, powder-coated
exterior finish.

## Care

The Breeze Tumbler is **dishwasher safe** (top rack recommended). For
best results, remove the lid before washing, since the lid's silicone
seal can loosen with repeated dishwasher cycles.
