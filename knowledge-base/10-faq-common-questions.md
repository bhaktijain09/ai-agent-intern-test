# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you offer gift wrapping?

Not currently. We include a branded card with every order at no
charge, but gift wrapping is not offered as a checkout option.

## Can I use multiple promo codes on one order?

No. Only one promotional code can be applied per order, and it cannot
be combined with the Row+ member discount beyond the single stacking
exception described in the membership benefits document.

## Do you price match?

Aster & Row does not price match competitor pricing. We do offer a
one-time price adjustment within 7 days of purchase if an item you
bought at full price goes on sale.

## How do I change my email address on file?

Email address changes must go through support and require identity
verification; they cannot be self-served from account settings today.

## What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards, PayPal, and Aster & Row gift cards. We
do not currently accept Afterpay or other buy-now-pay-later services.
