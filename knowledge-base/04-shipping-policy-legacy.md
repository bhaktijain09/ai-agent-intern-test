# Shipping Policy (Legacy — Superseded)

**This document is retained for historical reference only. Current
shipping rates and timelines are in 03-shipping-policy-current.md.**

## Old domestic shipping

Standard shipping took 5–9 business days and cost $7.95, with free
shipping only on orders over $100. Row Rewards members (the old name
for Row+) did not get free shipping — they got a flat discount of
$2.00 per order instead.

## Old carriers

At the time, orders were shipped exclusively via USPS. UPS was added
as a carrier option in the 2026 logistics overhaul.
