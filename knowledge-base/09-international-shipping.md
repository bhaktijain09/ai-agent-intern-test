# International Shipping

## Where we ship

Aster & Row currently ships to the United States, Canada, and the
United Kingdom. We do not yet ship to other countries.

## Canada

Standard shipping to Canada takes 7–12 business days and costs
$14.95, free on orders over $150. Canadian orders may be subject to
duties and taxes collected on delivery, which are the customer's
responsibility and are not included in the order total.

## United Kingdom

Standard shipping to the UK takes 8–14 business days and costs
$19.95. Aster & Row now collects UK VAT at checkout for orders under
£135, so no additional charges should be collected on delivery for
those orders.

## Returns on international orders

International customers follow the same return window as domestic
customers (see Returns Policy) but are responsible for return
shipping costs, which are not covered even for Row+ members.
